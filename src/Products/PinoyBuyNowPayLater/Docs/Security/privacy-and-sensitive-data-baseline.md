# Privacy and Sensitive Data Baseline

**Status:** Planning baseline (BNPL-00)  
**Implementation present:** No  
**Related:** BNPL-D-00-19, BNPL-D-00-20

## Defaults

- **No PHI** unless a future product decision explicitly authorizes health data (not expected for BNPL).  
- Financial balances, repayment history, and contact data are **sensitive commercial data**.  
- Least privilege for staff visibility; customer self-scope for Personal UX.  

## Open policy

Retention, deletion, export, and cross-border processing rules are Open (BNPL-D-00-19). Safe default: retain while organization subscribed; no silent purge of financial evidence.

## Regulatory note

Privacy/data-processing obligations may apply under Philippine law. This baseline does not constitute legal advice or a compliance certification.
