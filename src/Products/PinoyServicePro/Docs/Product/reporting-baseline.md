# Reporting Baseline

**Status:** Planning baseline (PSP-00)  
**Implementation present:** No

## Intent

Operational reports for service businesses, grant-scoped and branch-aware when multi-branch is enabled.

## Candidate report areas

- Bookings / utilization / no-shows
- Jobs completed / WIP
- Revenue by service / staff (commission reporting only if PSP-D-00-08 enables)
- Payments collected
- Estimates pending approval
- Asset / vehicle service summaries when capability enabled

Exact report catalog: **Open / Product Owner Decision Required**.

## Non-claims

Reports must not be marketed as BIR/tax/accounting compliance artifacts unless separately authorized (PSP-D-00-16).
