# 01 — Website Vision

## What exits.ph Is

exits.ph is the public marketing website for **ExItS** — a Philippine-focused SaaS platform that offers multiple independently subscribed business products under a unified identity and account infrastructure.

The website is a **public presentation and conversion surface**. It is not part of the SaaS application itself. It does not duplicate the platform's authentication, account management, billing, or any product-operational logic.

---

## Brand Positioning

| Element | Statement |
|---|---|
| What ExItS is | A multi-product SaaS platform built for Filipino businesses |
| Primary product | Pinoy Business POS — point-of-sale and business management |
| Platform promise | One account, multiple business tools, built for the Philippines |
| Tone | Professional, clear, approachable — not corporate cold, not startup gimmicky |
| Language | Filipino English; avoid jargon; use terms familiar to Filipino SME owners |

ExItS is **not**:
- A single-product startup
- A generic white-label POS
- An event platform
- A lifestyle brand

---

## Website Goals (Priority Order)

1. **Educate** — explain what ExItS and its products actually do.
2. **Convert** — guide qualified visitors to sign up or request information.
3. **Trust** — establish credibility for Philippine SME owners.
4. **Represent the product ecosystem** — surface all products honestly with correct readiness.
5. **Surface the growth story** — single location → multi-branch → multi-product.

---

## Primary Audience

| Segment | Description |
|---|---|
| Solo / micro business owners | Sari-sari, food stall, small retail — first digital POS |
| Growing SME owners | Multi-branch retail considering a platform upgrade |
| Business managers | Staff managing operations on behalf of an owner |
| Prospective investors / partners | Evaluating ExItS as a platform |

---

## Success Indicators

- Visitor understands what Pinoy Business POS does within 30 seconds on the homepage.
- Visitor can locate pricing information without confusion.
- Visitor reaches a sign-up or contact step with a single click from any product page.
- Product readiness is clearly communicated — no false promises.

---

## What the Website Must NOT Do

- Claim products are live/available when they are not.
- Invent numeric usage statistics, customer counts, or transaction volumes.
- Duplicate SaaS authentication, account, or billing logic in Next.js.
- Hard-code pricing without Product Owner approval.
- Show testimonials, case studies, or logos without real authorization.
- Claim regulatory compliance (e.g., BIR, SEC, BSP) without legal verification.
