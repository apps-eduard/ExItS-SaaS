# 03 — Brand and Messaging

**ExItS** — use this exact casing in all website content.
Do not write "Exits", "EXITS", or "exits" in public-facing copy.

Product names follow the same rule:
- **Pinoy Business POS** (not "POS", not "PinoyBusinessPOS")
- **Pinoy Service Pro** (not "PSP")

## Brand Voice

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| Clear | Plain language; no tech jargon in marketing copy |
| Confident | State capabilities directly; no hedging on confirmed features |
| Honest | Never overstate readiness; label "coming soon" clearly |
| Filipino-grounded | Use familiar SME business terms in the Philippine context |
| Professional | Not corporate-cold; approachable without being casual |

## Copy Rules

1. **Never invent numbers.** No fake customer counts, transaction volumes, or uptime claims.
2. **Capability statements over statistics.** Say "manage multiple branches" not "over 500 branches served."
3. **Label unconfirmed features.** "Coming soon" or "In development" must be visible, not hidden.
4. **Filipino English.** Avoid Americanisms that don't resonate locally; avoid pure Filipino (Tagalog) in main copy unless specifically authorized.
5. **Short sentences.** Marketing copy should be scannable. Max 2 sentences per bullet.
6. **Active voice.** "Manage your stock" not "Stock can be managed."
7. **No legal/compliance claims** (e.g., BIR-accredited, BSP-regulated) without legal sign-off.
8. **No testimonials** without real, consented quotes.

## CTA Language

Primary CTAs:
- "Get Started" — primary conversion
- "Request a Demo" — qualified lead
- "Learn More" — secondary discovery

Avoid:
- "Buy Now" (incorrect for SaaS signup)
- "Download" (unless a mobile app is confirmed available)
- "Free Trial" (unless pricing/trial policy is confirmed — TBD WEB-D-01)

---

## Prohibited Claims

Do not write, imply, or suggest:

- That any product other than Pinoy Business POS is currently live
- Specific ₱ prices without Product Owner approval
- Regulatory compliance (BIR, SEC, BSP, PCI-DSS) without legal confirmation
- Customer testimonials without real, authorized quotes
- Usage metrics without verified data
- That ExItS is the "leading" or "#1" anything without substantiation
